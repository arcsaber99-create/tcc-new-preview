Fat reduction

Botulinum toxin

Korean and US products. Published programmes include upper face, V-shape, lifting, neck, sweating, calves and back.

 03

Filler

Hyaluronic acid gels for contours such as the chin, under-eye area and other agreed sites.

Thread lift

Young Adult threads and Baby Face threads, used to lift and to encourage collagen.

Facial treatment
