Services

Doctor-led Botox (botulinum toxin) and hyaluronic acid filler, including lip filler, plus thread lift, HIFU, laser and vitamin drips. The clinic also offers medical certificates.

Fat reduction

Fat Bomb for S and M faces, from 10 cc, and Fat Tornado for L faces and the body.

 Illustrative image

Botulinum toxin (Botox)

Korean and US products for the upper face, V-shape, jaw, nasal ala, neck, underarms, calves and back.

 Illustrative image

Hyaluronic acid filler

Lip filler and hyaluronic acid for temples, under-eyes, cheeks, folds, forehead and chin.

Thread lift

Barbed (cog) threads for marked laxity, and Fine smooth threads for milder looseness.

Facial treatment

Acne extraction and injections, Cooling & Relax, phonophoresis, Melt & Lift, lymphatic pressure-point massage, masks and LED.

Laser

Brightening, spot and hair lasers. Other programmes are confirmed with the doctor in consultation.

HIFU

Focused ultrasound at the SMAS layer for half the face, the full face or the neck.

 Illustrative image

Intradermal vitamins

White Glow, White Full, White Detox and PRP prepared from your own blood.

Vitamin drip

Six formulas: Light Shot, Triple Shot, Fat on Fire, NAD+, Premium White and Hangover.

Moles and acne scars

Spot Damage for electrosurgery, Cube Clear for deep scars, and Smooth Face.

Medical certificates

For work, study, a Thai driving licence and visas, issued by the doctor after an examination.
