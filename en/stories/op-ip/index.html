Patient story

Op-Ip, on revision rhinoplasty

Op-Ip wrote her account in Thai. This is a translation. It is one operation, not a template.

Photo to be replaced

Photographs from Op-Ip’s story, to be supplied by the clinic

Op-Ip had already had a nose operation elsewhere. She felt the bridge was too thin and sharp, and friends remarked on it. She wanted a softer line. A friend who had surgery at The Cocktail Clinic suggested she visit.

Dr. Kony told her a revision was possible: a sloped bridge with a projected tip, which the doctor thought would suit her better, with cartilage from behind the ear used to support the tip so it would not push through.

On the day, the skin was cleaned and the silicone was carved. She was told this shaping takes a little time so the implant matches the nose, and that ear cartilage would reinforce the tip. Local anaesthetic stung briefly, then the nose was numb. She was awake and could tell work was happening, without pain. It took about an hour and a half.

When the stitches were closed she looked in the mirror. Nurses checked whether she was sore and explained the aftercare. The clinic also phoned afterwards to ask how she was. She wrote that she would send someone who is unsure where to have a nose done, or redone, to the clinic.

This page restates information the clinic has already published. It is general information, not personal medical advice. A consultation comes before any procedure.
