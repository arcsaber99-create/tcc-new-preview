Patient story

Som, on filler

This is Som’s account of her visit. It is one person’s experience, not a result the clinic promises to repeat.

Photographs from the clinic

Case photographs that are still available on the current website. Results differ from person to person.

Som describes her face as slightly oval, with a short chin, and says a V-shape was what she wanted. She had never felt fully at ease in photographs. Friends pointed her to The Cocktail Clinic. She wrote that the doctor seemed experienced and approachable, and that the products and the fees seemed fair to her.

She started on LINE at @thecocktailclinic. The staff answered, and she felt the advice was straightforward rather than a push to buy. She did not want surgery, because it would be permanent. Dr. Kony suggested filler, and explained that hyaluronic acid can be adjusted if the result is not what someone wants.

She was nervous. She wrote that Dr. Kony’s explanation put her at ease, that the steps were talked through, and that it hurt less than she had expected. She could see a difference immediately, and the doctor kept checking whether she was happy with it.

The aftercare she was given: no alcohol, no raw meat and no fermented food for a week. Later, people asked what had changed. She was pleased with the shape and has recommended the clinic to others.

This page restates information the clinic has already published. It is general information, not personal medical advice. A consultation comes before any procedure.
