Patient story

Mi, on PRP

Mi’s page is in Thai on the current site, including the English version. This is her account in English. One visit, one person.

Photo to be replaced

Photographs from Mi’s page are no longer on the server

Mi had Bloody PRP at the Phayathai clinic. She described dry skin that did not look clear and makeup that would not sit. Friends suggested the clinic. She wrote that Dr. Kony was kind, explained the treatment, and had a light hand.

Blood was taken and spun. She understood that using her own blood meant her body was unlikely to reject it. Numbing cream went on the face because there would be many injection points. The doctor then injected the spun blood. Mi wrote that she barely felt the needle. Staff looked after her. The doctor also mentioned that PRP can be placed in the scalp, where it is used in the hope of thicker hair.

The next day she felt her skin looked smoother and clearer, without swelling or bruising. She planned to repeat it about every two weeks. People commented, and she has recommended the clinic to anyone curious about PRP.

This page restates information the clinic has already published. It is general information, not personal medical advice. A consultation comes before any procedure.
