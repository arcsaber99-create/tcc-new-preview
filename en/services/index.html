Services

What the clinic offers

The list below is what the clinic has published. A doctor decides what fits, and what should wait. Results vary from person to person.

01

Fat reduction

Fat Bomb for S and M faces, from 10 cc, and Fat Tornado for L faces and the body.

 02

Botulinum toxin

Korean and US products for the upper face, V-shape, jaw, nasal ala, neck, underarms, calves and back.

 03

Filler

Hyaluronic acid for temples, under-eyes, cheeks, folds, forehead, lips and chin.

 04

Thread lift

Young Adult threads for marked laxity, and Baby Face threads for milder looseness.

 05

Facial treatment

Acne extraction and injections, Cooling & Relax, phonophoresis, Melt & Lift, lymphatic massage, masks and LED.

 06

Laser

Brightening, spot and hair lasers. The English menu also names programmes that are confirmed in consultation.

 07

HIFU

Focused ultrasound at the SMAS layer for half the face, the full face or the neck.

 08

Intradermal vitamins

White Glow, White Full, White Detox, Spot Reduce and PRP prepared from your own blood.

 09

Vitamin drip

Five formulas on the detail page: Light Shot, Triple Shot, Fat on Fire, NAD+ and Premium White.

 10

Moles and acne scars

Spot Damage for electrosurgery, Cube Clear for deep scars, and Smooth Face.

This page restates information the clinic has already published. It is general information, not personal medical advice. A doctor’s consultation is required before any procedure. Results vary from person to person, and nothing here is a promise of a particular result.
