Services

Intradermal vitamins

Skin-injection programmes include vitamins, collagen and plasma from your own blood. Depth and interval differ. The doctor chooses after looking at the skin.

The formulas that are described

White Glow

A multivitamin aimed at brightness and dark marks, placed by mesotherapy across the face and into problem areas. The doctor may nick the surface, go under the skin, or reach the fat layer. It does not sting, but it is watery, so swelling lasts about 30 minutes.

Needle marks can remain up to 24–48 hours. Bruises can be cooled or treated with Reparil gel.

White Full

Forty-one skin nutrients, with a filling effect shorter than gel filler, about 1–2 months. It is offered to people who want under-eye volume but are not ready for a gel. The clinic says this formula does not block a vessel the way a gel can, and that it also feeds dull under-eyes. That is the published reason, not a guarantee of zero risk.

A small sting at the needle, swelling for about 1–3 hours. A deep tear trough absorbs it faster and the swelling settles sooner. Every 2 weeks for the first 3 visits, then monthly. Bruising varies. Cold or a bruise gel can be used.

White Detox

Collagen with multivitamin, in 16 points along facial lymph areas. The clinic calls it a skin detox, aimed at reactive skin, heat rash, acne, pores, faster fading of red and dark marks, and makeup that sits more easily. The change it describes can start the next day.

Weekly for 4 weeks, then twice a month, then monthly. Very dull skin can return to the starting rhythm.

Spot Reduce

A formula aimed at melanin, so acne marks and melasma look lighter. A small change may show within 7 days, and a clearer one after 4 or more weekly visits. Numbing cream or ice can be used. The doctor concentrates on the marks. Swelling lasts about 30 minutes.

No specific restriction is listed afterwards. Around day 3 a mark can look slightly darker because the skin is dry. Moisturiser and sunscreen matter. Some dark marks are paired with a pigment medicine for at least a month. Spot Reduce Mini is named on the menu and is not described separately.

Bloody Baby PRP

PRP is platelet-rich plasma. Blood is drawn and spun, red cells are set aside, and the plasma with concentrated platelets is used. This fraction contains a stem-cell component. On the face the aims are skin cells, immune support, red and dark marks, inflammation, acne, pores and a smoother surface. On the scalp the aim is thicker-looking hair.

Numbing cream first, then injections across the face with extra attention to marks. It does not sting. Mild swelling along the points settles in about an hour, fullness lasts about 1–2 weeks, and clarity may remain after that. Every 3–4 weeks for about 2–3 sessions. It is your own blood, so no outside product is added. Suitability is still screened. That does not mean a reaction is impossible.

Questions

Does White Full replace filler?

It is shorter, about 1–2 months, and is offered to people who want to try under-eye volume before a gel. The doctor compares the two.

Whose blood is used for PRP?

Yours. After spinning, the plasma fraction is used. It is not mixed with a product from someone else.

This information is provided for general knowledge and is not personal medical advice. A doctor’s consultation is required before any procedure. Results vary from person to person, and nothing here is a promise of a particular result.
