Facial treatment and acne

Skin treatments take about 30–40 minutes, and makeup can usually go on afterwards. Acne care includes extraction, injections and home medicine. The doctor sets the course.

Overview

The services menu names extraction, acne injections, AHA, phonophoresis, facial massage, Cooling & Relax, lymphatic massage, Melt & Lift, a facial mask, a gold mask, acne treatment and light therapy. The programmes below are the ones described in full. AHA is named but not given its own method, so it is confirmed in consultation.

Several programmes can be repeated weekly. The clinic writes that they are not intended to irritate or harm the skin. That is not a promise that nobody will react.

Facial programmes

Cooling & Relax

Cryotherapy cold is used to work skincare in more deeply than a cream or a mask, and to lower skin temperature. The aims are less inflammation and reactivity, a calmer and more hydrated feel, and red or dark marks that fade sooner.

The buffet of ingredients is aloe vera, arbutin, a botulinum serum, collagen and hyaluronic acid. It can be done every week.

Phonophoresis

High-frequency sound pushes the same ingredients deeper than Cooling & Relax. The aims are similar: calmer, less reactive, more hydrated skin, and care of red or dark marks. Weekly.

Melt & Lift

Warm radiofrequency is massaged where it is needed, with the aims of lift, fat, lines and a fresher surface. Weekly.

Lymphatic massage

Hands work the lymph points of the face to ease muscle, support lymph flow and the immune response. It also supports circulation and acne care. Weekly.

Anti-allergic mask

A hydrating mask for skin tired by sun and pollution, aimed at a fresher look and at acne, rash and redness from inflammation.

Gold mask

A pure-gold mask aimed at renewal, collagen, hydration, brightness, lines and new skin cells.

Light therapy

LED wavelengths are chosen for the aim, including bacteria linked with acne, dark marks and collagen. A session is 30–40 minutes, weekly, with 4 continuous weeks suggested. No special preparation, it is not painful, and you can carry on afterwards.

How acne is approached

Acne is described as open and closed comedones. Care starts with a dissolving cream and extraction every 1–2 weeks, to lift comedones that could inflame later. Inflamed spots can be injected once a week to reduce swelling and redness. Home creams cover both dissolving lesions and bacteria.

Reduce dust from bedding and curtains by washing them often, wash the face with drinking water rather than groundwater, change makeup that clogs pores, keep using sunscreen, and use aloe-based care.

Questions

Can I wear makeup and go into the sun?

A facial is 30–40 minutes, with no planned downtime, and makeup can go on afterwards. Sunscreen still matters, especially after laser or light.

How do the acne courses differ?

Regular is the doctor visit, extraction, injection of inflamed spots and one home product. Five visits include one brightening laser. Jumbo adds a vitamin acid, light therapy and three home products each time.
