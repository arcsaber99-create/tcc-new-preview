Vitamin drip

The detail page describes five formulas. A doctor decides which, if any, is suitable. This is not a treatment for heart disease or a substitute for specialist care.

Book on LINE Free consultation

Five formulas

Light Shot

A multivitamin with vitamin C 4,000 mg and vitamins B1, B2, B3, B6 and B12. The clinic lists brightness, general health, antioxidant support, collagen, immunity, dark spots, softer skin, wound healing, allergy symptoms, fatigue, nerves and numbness among the aims.

Triple Shot

A multivitamin about five times the concentration of Light Shot, plus collagen and Q10, aimed at a lighter skin tone.

Fat on Fire

Built around L-carnitine and B vitamins, aimed at using fat as energy. It is a clinic formula and can be done weekly. The English page describes the same idea as metabolism.

NAD+

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a derivative of vitamin B3. The clinic lists support for the body, antioxidant effect, nerves, memory, vision, hearing, mental fatigue, energy, clearance of unwanted substances, fat in blood vessels, and exercise tolerance.

Premium White

The published ingredients are placenta, whitening 1,200 mg, alpha lipoic acid 25 mg, tranexamic acid, vitamin C 20,000 mg, cyanocobalamin 2,000 mcg, thiamine 400 mg, riboflavin 1 mg, nicotinamide 200 mg, pyridoxine HCl 102 mg, amino acids and a multivitamin.

The listed aims cover sleep, memory, menopausal restlessness, sexual function, a lighter skin tone, dark spots, lines, oil balance, dryness, skin allergy, immunity and the nervous system. The original text also mentions cardiovascular risk. The clinic does not offer this drip as a treatment for heart disease.

Names on the services menu

The Thai services menu also named Avengers Vitamin and bottom up beside Light Shot and Triple Shot. The detail page does not describe that formula, so its ingredients are not guessed. The English menu names only Light Shot and Triple Shot.

Preparation and timing

  • Sleep enough beforehand. The English page suggests at least 6 hours, and plenty of water.
  • Afterwards, drink plenty of water. Usual skincare is fine.
  • Choose a slow drip of about 30 minutes or a slow push of about 15 minutes.

Questions

How long does it take?

About 30 minutes for a slow drip, or about 15 minutes for a slow push, not including the consultation.

How often is Fat on Fire?

The clinic says it can be weekly. The doctor still decides whether that rhythm suits you.
