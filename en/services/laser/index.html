Laser

Laser

The process

  1. 01

    Super Bright

    An IPL treatment aimed at marks from acne or sun, working on the surface so the clinic says the skin is not made thinner. It can be repeated every two weeks. A change may be noticeable from about seven days, and four sessions in a row is the published suggestion.

  2. 02

    Stay out of the sun for at least a week afterwards and use sunscreen. Pregnancy is listed as a reason not to have this treatment.

  3. 03

    Spot removal

    A Q-switched laser for dark spots and uneven pigment, including marks left by acne. The clinic also describes brighter skin, smaller-looking pores and collagen activity. Again, about seven days is when a change may show.

  4. 04

    The published course is every two weeks for four or five sessions, and strong sun is best avoided for three days. Not recommended in pregnancy.

  5. 05

    Unclouded Hair

    Hair reduction. S covers facial hair. M covers the face, an armpit, one arm or one leg. The clinic calls the change partly long-lasting and suggests a visit every four weeks, for five to eight sessions, once hair is about 1 mm long. A change may show in four or five days. Thicker hair can need more sessions than fine hair. The published expectation is that the effect lasts about three to four years.

  6. 06

    Named, but not described in detail

    Deep Down, Deep Plus, Deep Plus Mini and tattoo removal are also offered. How they differ is discussed in a consultation rather than spelled out here.
