Injection

Filler

Smooth, colourless gels of hyaluronic acid, placed at different depths to soften lines or to reshape a contour such as the chin or the under-eye area.

The process

  1. 01

    What it is

    Hyaluronic acid is already in skin, where it has a part in softness and volume. Levels fall with age, which is one reason lines and looseness appear. A filler visit is described as taking a few minutes, with a change that can be seen at once and little recovery time.

  2. 02

    The clinic uses the gels for moderate to more noticeable correction of lines, creases and contours. Products differ in concentration and in the layer of skin they are placed in. A cross-linked structure is what the clinic points to when it talks about how long a result lasts.

  3. 03

    How a visit is described

    Placement follows a sequence aimed at balance. The gels used here contain an anaesthetic. The clinic also states that its hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved, which is how it frames the safety of the choice.

  4. 04

    Redness around the injection can last an hour or so. Bruising is always possible with injections. The published advice is to skip alcohol, omega-3 products and anti-inflammatory drugs for 24 hours beforehand.
