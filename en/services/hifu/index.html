Lift

HIFU

High-intensity focused ultrasound aimed at the SMAS, the muscle layer just above the bone. The clinic offers it for half the face, the full face, or the neck.

The process

  1. 01

    The ultrasound is described as making very small points of injury that cannot be seen. The clinic’s account is that the body lays down collagen as those points settle, the muscle feels tighter, and the contour sits higher. Skin renewal is also part of how the page describes the effect.

  2. 02

    The treatment can be uncomfortable, so numbing cream is used.

  3. 03

    The published course is at least two sessions in the first month and a third about two months later. After that course, the clinic describes the effect as lasting about a year. That is the clinic’s published expectation for this plan, not a promise that every face will match it.
