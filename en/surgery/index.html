Surgery

Surgery the clinic has published

Four procedures appear on the original surgery page. Nose surgery is described in full. Chin, eyelids and fat grafting are summarised from what was published, and still need a consultation.

01

Nose surgery

Silicone can be chosen by shape and grade. The clinic uses American silicone and carves every piece to the face.

 02

Chin surgery

Aimed at a longer, slimmer line, and at a crooked, square or short chin. The English page mentions both an implant and injection.

 03

Double eyelid

Aimed at a larger, softer eye for small or single eyelids, stitched to look natural.

 04

Facial fat graft

Your own fat, placed in the face for fullness and collagen. Also described for small soft-tissue hollows of the face and jaw.

Chin

The Thai page says chin surgery is used for a longer, slimmer face and for a crooked, square or short chin. The English page adds that the chin can be reshaped, enlarged or brought forward, by surgery or by injection. Surgery places a plastic material and fixes it to the bone.

There is no separate detail page. Shape, material and recovery are discussed with the doctor.

Double eyelid

The aim is a larger, softer eye for a small eye or a single lid, with a stitch that looks natural. The English page notes that many people who ask are Asian and have a single lid, and have used makeup or tape to suggest a crease.

Facial fat graft

Fat from your own body is placed in the face. The published aims are fullness, collagen and a more hydrated look. The English page says the clinic has used it for small soft-tissue hollows of the face and jaw, and mentions the amount of tissue available, limited trouble at the donor site, and tissue that settles into place.

This page restates information the clinic has already published. It is general information, not personal medical advice. A doctor’s consultation is required before any procedure. Results vary from person to person, and nothing here is a promise of a particular result.
