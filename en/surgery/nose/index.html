Surgery

Nose surgery

The nose sits in the centre of the face, so a change is easy to see. Shape is agreed with the doctor first. The clinic uses American silicone, described as natural and not overly firm, and carves every piece.

Book on LINE Free consultation

What is agreed first

Shape is assessed with the doctor so both sides share the same picture. The English surgery overview also mentions medical reasons such as breathing, injury or a shape present from birth. Those questions belong in the consultation. They do not mean every case is treatment of a disease.

Before surgery

  • Declare medical conditions, regular medicines such as aspirin or ibuprofen, allergies, any filler in the nose, and previous surgery in detail.
  • Stop supplements, collagen, vitamins, painkillers and alcohol for 2 weeks. The English page names vitamin C in that list.
  • The English page also suggests at least 8 hours of sleep.

During

About 1–2 hours. You can go home the same day.

After surgery

  • Cold compress for 2 weeks beside the nose, between the brows and under the eyes, and longer if swelling remains.
  • Keep the head up on two pillows for 2 days. The first night can be extra pillows or a recliner. Sleep on your back, not your side, for 1 month.
  • If packing or splints are inside the nose, do not pull them out. If they clog, breathe through your mouth. An external splint stays 5 days, and can come off from day 3 if it itches.
  • A little bleeding for 2–3 days can be normal. Call the doctor if it is heavy. Do not blow your nose for at least a week. Sneeze with your mouth open.
  • Light exercise waits 2 weeks. Heavy exercise, or lifting more than 3 kg, waits 4 weeks.
  • Do not wash the face, as the clinic instructs. Do not pick or reach inside the nose.
  • Clean the wound gently each day with saline and a cotton bud. Scabs can be wiped, not pulled. Dry the wound and apply antibiotic ointment.
  • Soft food, and no hot food, for 3 days. No raw food, fermented food, salty food, spicy food or alcohol for 1 month.
  • No glasses with arms for 4 weeks. No aspirin afterwards, because it slows clotting.
  • Finish the prescribed medicine. Sutures come out at 7–14 days.

Questions

Can I go home the same day?

Yes, as the clinic has published. You still need a way home and the wound-care list.

When do the sutures come out?

Between 7 and 14 days.

When can I wear glasses?

Glasses with arms wait 4 weeks.
